# GTM.md (Go-To-Market Strategy)

## Product Positioning
- **Tagline**: Stop Overspending on AI Tools.
- **Subheadline**: Audit your AI stack in 60 seconds and uncover hidden monthly savings.
- **Analogy**: "Mint for AI Spend" / "FinOps for AI Tooling"

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Startup Founders (Seed to Series B) and CTOs looking to extend runway.
- **Secondary**: Engineering Managers realizing their teams are double-billing for Cursor and Copilot.

## Acquisition Channels
1. **Product Hunt Launch**: SpendScope AI is designed to be highly viral and screenshot-worthy. The core loop (Audit -> Massive Savings -> Share Report) is optimized for PH traffic.
2. **X / Twitter**: Founders sharing their "AI Stack Audit" results via the public URL generation feature. We will generate Twitter cards with dynamic Open Graph images showing "I just saved $X/mo on AI".
3. **Hacker News**: "Show HN: We built a tool to show how much you're wasting on AI subscriptions."
4. **Partner Channel (Credex)**: Credex uses this tool as a lead magnet. "Audit your spend, and if it's over $500, we'll consult you on getting $100k in cloud credits."

## The Viral Loop
1. User sees someone else's Audit Report on Twitter.
2. Clicks through to `spendscope.ai`.
3. Fills out the completely free, no-login 60-second form.
4. Sees massive "shock value" savings.
5. Captures email to download the PDF.
6. Shares their own link.
