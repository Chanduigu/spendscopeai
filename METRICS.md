# METRICS.md

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

To measure the success of SpendScope AI's launch and ongoing operations, we track the following metrics:

### 1. Acquisition & Top of Funnel
- **Unique Visitors:** Total traffic to the landing page.
- **Audit Start Rate:** % of visitors who click "Start Free Audit".
- **Form Completion Rate:** % of users who finish Step 2 and reach the "Analyzing" state. (Target: >80% due to friction-free UX).

### 2. Conversion & Lead Generation
- **Email Capture Rate:** % of users on the Results page who input their email to "Share & Export Report". (Target: >30% due to gated PDF value).
- **Consultation Booking Rate:** % of qualified users (spend >$500/mo) who click "Book Credex Consultation".

### 3. Engine Performance
- **Average Savings Found:** The average dollar amount the engine identifies in savings per user. High numbers increase virality.
- **Percentage Optimized:** What % of teams are already perfectly optimized vs needing changes.

### 4. Virality
- **Share Link Clicks:** Number of hits on the generated public URL reports.
- **K-Factor:** How many new unique visitors does each shared report generate? (Target: >0.5).
